### Abstract

Oneway functions are the functions that are easy to compute but hard to invert. In terms of oracle Turing machines, we can formalize the notion of computable functions from reals to reals, and define it to be oneway if no probabilistic algorithm can invert it on a positive measure set. We show that there is a total computable randompreserving surjective oneway function. Then, we explore other possible formulations of oneway functions and discuss their relation. We also try to characterize the oracle power needed to compute a probabilistic inversion of every computable function. Finally, we study the case where additional properties like being injective and being collision-resistant are imposed.
